# Sendspark

> Sendspark is an AI-personalized video platform for B2B sales, marketing, and customer success teams. Users record one video, then Sendspark uses AI voice cloning and dynamic backgrounds to generate thousands of individually personalized versions — each with the recipient's name, company, LinkedIn profile, or website as the video background. Videos are delivered via email, LinkedIn, or CRM workflows with per-viewer analytics.

- Type: SaaS WebApplication
- Category: Sales Engagement, Video Personalization
- Audience: B2B sales teams, SDRs, AEs, marketers, customer success managers, agencies
- Pricing: Solo $49/mo, Growth $99/mo, Team $299/mo, Business $699/mo, Enterprise custom. 7-day free trial, no credit card required.
- Headquarters: Austin, TX
